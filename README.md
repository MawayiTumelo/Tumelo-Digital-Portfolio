# Tumelo Veli Mawayi — Personal & Technical Portfolio

A high-performance, brutalist technical portfolio and digital showcase for **Tumelo Veli Mawayi**, Founder & Systems Architect at **Nexlink Solutions ZA** (South Africa). 

This web application highlights cutting-edge South African technology initiatives across AI education, public transportation infrastructure, and municipal utility telemetry.

---

## 🌟 Overview

The platform serves as an interactive portfolio and project registry designed with a sleek brutalist aesthetic, typographic precision, high-contrast dark/light mode themes, and immersive real-time UI components.

### Core Objectives
1. **Showcase High-Impact Projects**: Present key engineering initiatives addressing critical socio-economic challenges in South Africa.
2. **Interactive Technical Deep-Dives**: Provide comprehensive modals detailing the architecture, technical challenges, solutions, and impact metrics for each project.
3. **Facilitate Partner Inquiries**: Enable prospective collaborators, investors, and clients to send direct inquiry briefings to Nexlink Solutions ZA.

## 🚀 Key Projects Featured

### 1. ClassBridge AI
* **Category**: AI / LLM • CAPS Curriculum • EdTech
* **Description**: A CAPS-aligned educator continuity and AI substitute lesson platform. When subject educators are absent, school administrators activate **Continuity Mode** to instantly generate structured, interactive substitute lessons mapped directly to South Africa's official national curriculum.
* **Impact**: Prevents lost classroom hours and maintains continuous learning in under-resourced schools.

### 2. Siyaya
* **Category**: Real-Time GPS • WebSockets • Commuter Transit Tech
* **Description**: A South African minibus taxi ecosystem connector linking commuters, rank marshals, and drivers. Features real-time seat availability indicators (check if full before walking to the rank), live route tracking, passenger feedback, and instant safety/harassment incident logging.
* **Impact**: Restores route transparency and enhances passenger safety in daily urban commuting.

### 3. PowerGrid ZA
* **Category**: IoT Telemetry • Municipal Utilities • Grid Analytics
* **Description**: A community utility monitoring engine built for South African municipal challenges. Tracks load-shedding electrical surges, water pipe bursts, and enables real-time local fault reporting and solar micro-grid sharing.
* **Impact**: Accelerates municipal repair response times and empowers communities with grid transparency.
